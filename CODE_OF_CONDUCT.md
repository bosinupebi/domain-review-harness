# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, assume good intent while taking impact seriously, and respect people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Critique work and ideas without attacking people.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and spaces where someone represents the project. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact route on their GitHub profile. If the owner is involved, contact GitHub Support. Do not publish identifying details in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request changes in conduct, limit participation, or remove access. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Reports will be handled with discretion, though absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Participants may ask the repository owner to reconsider an enforcement decision privately.
